ENGINEERING NOTES · OCTOBER 2026

The work behind the work.

A few things I've learned from building systems that act, integrate, and sometimes fail:

Model output is not business truth. Let AI propose; let deterministic systems authorize and commit.

Shipping is a verification problem. A convincing UI or green test isn't the same as a complete operating workflow.

The user is the real specification. Business processes are messy. Learn the real workflow before rebuilding it.

Make failure observable. Permissions, idempotency, retries, provenance, and rollback deserve first-class design.

— Deon
